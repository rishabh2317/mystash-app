## Rollback limits, cost and decision gates

- Disable new capture intake or its specific Find flags and stop incompatible writers to contain a failure; retain the upgraded schema, removal markers and private media/cleanup path. Rolling the shared database back in place or running old `2ad08bf` writers after these migrations can resurrect removed items or break newer writes. Restore from a fresh backup only through a separately reviewed recovery procedure that accounts for intervening writes and Storage objects. Render can redeploy a known good *schema-compatible* commit, not necessarily its current old commit.
- Free Render services can sleep, have constrained memory and volatile Redis; persistent Mac service availability depends on the Mac. Observe queue depth, failed jobs, memory, uploads, orphan cleanup and latency during QA. No paid Render upgrade or extra cloud worker is proposed.
- Expo Free shows 3 of 15 Android builds used for 2026-11-01 to 2026-12-01; one medium build would use the fourth and currently has no estimated build overage. Firebase/FCM setup has no proposed paid-tier change. Supabase Storage/egress, Vision, AI/search/place providers and network use can incur variable charges; the first smoke set is bounded to five captures. Do not run an open-ended extraction replay. Recheck quotas and billing before execution.
- Baseline full-suite failures remain 12 client assertions/modules and 3 backend assertions; no new failure was found in Phase 7A. Those do not alone block an internal QA APK. Auth/RLS/Storage, queue-to-Stash, native APK and physical-device checks are required to establish full functionality. Push delivery is a separate acceptance gate only for the FCM-enabled variant. Phase 7A measurement completeness and the known cloud YouTube downloader limitation are still open.

## Physical Android QA checklist

- Install over versionCode 4 with the same signing certificate. Confirm sign-in/session return, existing pending Share URL recovery, five-tab Watch · Search · Find · Stash · Profile navigation, Creator Studio from Profile, and unchanged product/creator/collection Search.
- Paste and submit supported URL while online and offline, then bring the app forward. Share Instagram Reel and YouTube Short through the redesigned native Android popup; repeat a tap and check deduplication, immediate secure-queue confirmation, accepted processing state, completion and accurate Recently Found/Needs Attention labels.
- Grant, deny and recover Gallery and Camera permissions. Capture a screenshot, an existing photo, and a new camera image. Check rotation, large images, progress, retries and that private media is visible only to its owner.
- For each capture, compare extracted products/places with automatically saved Stash items. Exercise partial and zero-result outcomes. Remove a result, wait for retry/reconciliation, confirm it stays removed; explicitly restore and confirm one saved membership. Remove a Find and check its expected Stash/media behavior.
- Repeat image upload through network loss, force-stop/restart, foreground resume, and phone reboot. Check there is no duplicate history/Stash entry. Sign out during a pending upload, switch to account B, and confirm B sees/delivers none of A's local or server data; sign back into A and verify permitted recovery.
- Test one completion notification for a multi-result capture, failure notification where applicable, permission denial, quiet-hours/preference/consent settings, foreground/background/cold-start tap, correct Find deep link and owner-only content. Check unrelated social/campaign notifications for regression.
- Inspect light and dark themes, safe areas, keyboard, font scaling, TalkBack, touch targets, loading/empty/error states, long histories/counts, and the upgraded native Share sheet on the target Android device. Record device/Android version, APK build ID, test account, timestamps, Find IDs and server/worker logs in private QA evidence (never in the repo).
